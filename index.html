錯誤詳細分析

1

基礎動詞與時態錯誤

原文寫 "trees that birds lived on in"，這裡介系詞重複且搭配錯誤。鳥住在樹上通常用 inhabit 或 live in trees。另外 "fish swam swim" 出現嚴重的時態與動詞重複錯誤。 "can be happened" 是常見錯誤，happen 是不及物動詞，沒有被動語態。

2

詞彙選擇不確定 (Indecisive Vocabulary)

原文出現 "competitiveness prospects/chances/the likelihood"，這在正式考試中是大忌。寫作時必須做出選擇，不能羅列同義詞。這裡建議用 "economic prospects"。

3

中式英語與搭配詞 (Collocations)

"makes a damages" 應改為 "causes damage" (damage 作為損害通常不可數)。"strike a balance on between" 介系詞多餘，應為 "strike a balance between A and B"。

結構診斷

  • 開頭段 (Introduction): 有改寫題目並提出雙方觀點，但第一句 "With the development of the countries" 略顯老套。
  • 主體段 1 (Environment): 論點主要集中在「破壞」的描述。建議增加「為什麼保護環境長期對人類有益」的論述，而不僅僅是說動物失去了家園。
  • 主體段 2 (Economy): "The more skyscrapers are built..." 邏輯過於跳躍。摩天大樓多不代表經濟好。應解釋基礎建設如何帶動商業活動。
  • 結論段 (Opinion): 提出了 "strike a balance" 的觀點，這很好。但具體建議（問專家、設保護區）寫得比較像解決方案 (Solution)，而非單純的論證。

7.0+ 範文段落重寫 (Body Paragraph 1)

"Proponents of environmental protection argue that natural habitats are often the unspoken casualties of rapid urbanization. As forests are cleared to make way for skyscrapers and infrastructure, biodiversity declines sharply. For instance, deforestation not only destroys the nesting grounds of avian species but also disrupts river ecosystems, leading to the disappearance of aquatic life. Furthermore, aggressive land reclamation can destabilize geological structures, resulting in natural disasters such as mudslides, which ultimately threaten both wildlife and human settlements."

學習亮點: unspoken casualties (無聲的受害者), rapid urbanization (快速都市化), destabilize (破壞穩定), biodiversity (生物多樣性)
